# Nexora AI - Monetization Strategy

> **Zero-investment revenue models** for a 24/7 chatbot service targeting Canadian HVAC/home services companies.

---

## 🎯 Core Philosophy

**You don't need a perfect product. You need conversations with prospects.**

The landing page is live. Now you need to sell. Every "no" teaches you the objection to handle next time.

---

## 💰 Revenue Model 1: Direct Service Sales (Highest Margin)

### The Offer
> "I'll set up a 24/7 chat on your site for 30 days FREE. If you don't get 3+ qualified leads, you pay nothing."

### Pricing (Monthly Retainer)
| Plan | Price | What You Do |
|------|-------|-------------|
| **Starter** | $299/mo | Configure Tawk.to/Crisp, write 10 rules, deliver leads via email |
| **Professional** | $699/mo | Unlimited rules, WhatsApp delivery, basic dashboard, priority support |
| **Business** | $1,499/mo | Conditional logic, webhooks, advanced reports, dedicated manager |

### Your Cost
- **$0** — Use free tiers: Tawk.to (free), Crisp (free tier), Chatwoot (open source)
- **Time**: ~2 hrs setup per client, ~30 min/week maintenance

### Margin
- **Starter**: $299/mo → ~95% profit
- **Professional**: $699/mo → ~95% profit
- **Business**: $1,499/mo → ~90% profit (more support time)

## 💰 Revenue Model 3: Lead Arbitrage

### The Concept
You run chat for multiple companies → qualify leads → sell to contractors who DON'T have chat.

### Example
- You manage chat for 5 HVAC companies in Toronto
- Get 50 leads/week across all
- 20 are "emergency AC repair" — high value
- Sell to 3 non-chat contractors at $50/lead = $1,000/week

### Legal/Ethical
- Disclose in chat: "We may share your request with trusted partners"
- Only sell to vetted, licensed contractors
- Canadian anti-spam law (CASL) compliance required

### Exit

## 📋 Legal/Compliance (Canada)

- **CASL**: Express consent for commercial emails → use double opt-in
- **PIPEDA**: Privacy policy required → use Termly free generator
- **Provincial**: Quebec Law 25 (similar to GDPR) if serving QC
- **Contracts**: Simple MSA template → Docracy free templates
- **Insurance**: E&O insurance ~$500/yr (optional at start)
