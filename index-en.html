FAQ

Everything you need to know

How does Nexora integrate with my website?

Just add one line of code to your site's . The widget appears in the bottom right. Works on WordPress, Wix, Shopify, plain HTML — any platform.

Can I customize the assistant's responses?

Yes. You define the rules: which questions to answer, what info to collect, when to escalate to a human. The dashboard lets you edit everything without code.

What happens if the assistant doesn't know the answer?

It flags the conversation as "needs human" and notifies your team instantly (WhatsApp/email). The client knows someone will follow up.

Do I need technical skills to set it up?

No. We do the initial config together on the demo call (15 min). After that, you edit rules in a visual Notion-like panel. Support included in all plans.

How do leads reach my team?

Formatted summary via WhatsApp, email, or web dashboard. Includes: name, phone, address, equipment, urgency, conversation history. Your team just schedules.

Does it work in French/English?

The widget detects browser language. Responses can be configured in multiple languages. Dashboard available in PT/EN.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. Month-to-month, cancel anytime. Setup is one-time (initial config). If you cancel and come back, no setup fee again.

Do you serve outside Canada?

Currently focused on the Canadian market (HVAC/home services). If you operate elsewhere, let's talk — we're expanding.
