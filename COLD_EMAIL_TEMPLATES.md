## Deliverability Checklist

- [ ] Custom domain email (not @gmail.com)
- [ ] SPF record: `v=spf1 include:_spf.google.com ~all` (or your provider)
- [ ] DKIM enabled
- [ ] DMARC: `v=DMARC1; p=quarantine; rua=mailto:dmarc@yourdomain.com`
- [ ] Warm up: 10/day week 1, 20/day week 2, 50/day week 3
- [ ] Unsubscribe link in footer (legal requirement)
- [ ] Physical address in footer (CASL compliance)
